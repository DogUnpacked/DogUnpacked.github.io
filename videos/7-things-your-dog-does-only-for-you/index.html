Your dog does seven things for one person in your house and nobody else. Most owners never notice — and the science behind each one is stranger than the behavior.

1. He follows you into the bathroom
2. He stares at you, and you stare back
3. He knows your smell from every human on Earth
4. He'd rather hear your voice than eat

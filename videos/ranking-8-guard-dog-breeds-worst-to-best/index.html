Not all dog breeds are created equal when it comes to home defense. This ranking separates the animals capable of genuine protection from those better suited for companionship, looking at everything from the police dog in training to the husky in the snow.
Effectiveness depends on intent and history. Whether you are looking for a dedicated working dog or just a companion that alerts you to visitors, understanding the difference is critical before considering any form of protection training. He is not broken if he cannot guard; he just was not bred that way.

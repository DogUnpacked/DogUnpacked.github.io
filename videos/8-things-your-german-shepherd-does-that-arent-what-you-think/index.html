Description

Your German Shepherd isn't anxious, clingy, or stubborn — he's working. Eight everyday German Shepherd behaviors, each one traced back to the job the breed was built for: tending herder, "living fence," one-handler working dog. Why he follows you from room to room (it's not separation anxiety), why he herds your kids, why he paces at 8 PM and the two-minute scent-work fix, why he's cold to strangers for exactly one minute, and the temperament-test number that starts arguments.

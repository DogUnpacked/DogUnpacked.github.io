Icelandic Sheepdog

The job

The Icelandic Sheepdog was developed to herd sheep and round up ponies.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Among what they brought from Norway were spitz-type dogs that Icelanders used for herding sheep and rounding up ponies.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. About 1,100 years ago, Norse settlers crossed the Norwegian Sea to Iceland.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The breed is Iceland's only native dog.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The AKC breed standard lists height as 18 inches (male), 16.5 inches (female) and weight as 30 pounds (male), 25 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The American Kennel Club lists 2010 as the year this breed was recognized.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that herding work can look like a dog who wants to keep the family together and move them along from room to room.

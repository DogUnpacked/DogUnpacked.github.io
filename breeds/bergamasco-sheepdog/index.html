Bergamasco Sheepdog

The job

Bergamascos were developed to move and guard sheep on rocky Alpine slopes.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The breed has been associated with the town of Bergamo, near Milan, for many centuries.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. They are thought to come from Persian stock.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. They were among breeds traded around the Mediterranean by the Phoenicians.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. They are suited to move and guard sheep on rocky Alpine slopes.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 23.5 inches (male), 22 inches (female) and weight as 70-84 pounds (male), 57-71 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house that sheepdog job can look like a dog who watches the household and likes to keep the family group together.

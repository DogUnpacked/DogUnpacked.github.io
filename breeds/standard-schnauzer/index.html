Facts

  1. The breed was first called Wire-Haired Pinschers and, by the 1900s, Schnauzers, a name that refers to the bearded muzzle.

  2. On farms the breed worked as ratters, herders, guardians, and hunters.

  3. Height 18.5-19.5 inches (male), 17.5-18.5 inches (female). Weight 35-50 pounds (male), 30-45 pounds (female).

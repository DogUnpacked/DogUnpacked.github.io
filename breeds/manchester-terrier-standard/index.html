Manchester Terrier (Standard)

The job

The Manchester Terrier was developed to hunt rabbits and to catch rats.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. In the mid-1800s, local mill workers hunted rabbits with small hounds and set terriers on rats.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Breeders crossed a ratter called the Black and Tan Terrier with Whippets and got a distinct Manchester type.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. Until 1959 the Standard and the Toy were registered as separate breeds, though interbreeding was permitted.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. Aside from size, the stated difference between the Standard and the Toy is ear shape.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 15-16 inches and weight as 12-22 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that work can look like a dog who notices small movement, wants to chase a scurry, and likes a fast run in an open space.

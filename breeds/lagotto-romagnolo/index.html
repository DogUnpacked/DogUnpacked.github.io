Lagotto Romagnolo

The job

The Lagotto Romagnolo was developed to retrieve waterfowl in the marshes, and later to find truffles by scent.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Lagotti go back to at least Renaissance Italy, where they were bred as waterfowl retrievers in the marshlands of Ravenna.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. The name is tied to water: Lago is Italian for lake.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. For many years they have been used in the Italian countryside to sniff out truffles.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The Lagotto is the only purebred dog recognized as a specialized truffle searcher.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 17-19 inches (male), 16-18 inches (female) and weight as 28.5-35 pounds (male), 24-31 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that nose work can look like a dog who wants to search, sniff along the ground, and bring back what it finds.

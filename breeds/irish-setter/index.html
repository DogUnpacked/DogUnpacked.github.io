Facts

  1. Solid red Irish Setters first appeared in Ireland in the 19th century.

  2. Before guns, setters worked with trained falcons and with hunters who used nets.

  3. Height 27 inches (male), 25 inches (female). Weight 70 pounds (male), 60 pounds (female).

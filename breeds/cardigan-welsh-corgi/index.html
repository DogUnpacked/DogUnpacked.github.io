Cardigan Welsh Corgi

The job

The Cardigan Welsh Corgi was developed to heel livestock, moving cattle by nipping at their heels.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The Cardigan is best known as a heeler, a dog that moves cows by nipping at their heels.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. The breed is named for the medieval kingdom of Cardiganshire in Wales.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The Cardigan comes from the same line of dogs as the Dachshund.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The two corgis were treated as one breed in Britain until 1934.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 10.5-12.5 inches and weight as 30-38 pounds (male), 25-34 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

Around the house, that heeling work can look like a dog who follows close behind and may nip at heels when everyone is on the move. A walk with a person to follow gives the old cattle job a quieter version.

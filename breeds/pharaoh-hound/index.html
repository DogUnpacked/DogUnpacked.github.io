Pharaoh Hound

The job

The Pharaoh Hound was developed in Malta to hunt rabbits.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Phoenician traders from what is today Lebanon sailed the Mediterranean about 2,500 years ago.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. The prevailing theory is that Phoenicians brought the Pharaoh Hound to Malta, where it was used to hunt rabbits.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. In 1979 the Pharaoh Hound was named the national hound of Malta.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. Phoenicians are assumed to have spread Egyptian hounds through the ancient world, as far west as Britain.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 23-25 inches (male), 21-24 inches (female) and weight as 45-55 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that hunting work can look like a dog who wants a chance to chase and who comes alive when something moves in the open.

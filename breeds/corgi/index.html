Herding Group

Pembroke Welsh Corgi

The job

The Pembroke Welsh Corgi was developed to herd cattle and sheep.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The best weavers in Europe lived in Flanders.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. In 1107 the king of Britain invited a community of weavers to live and work in Wales.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. They brought dogs used to herd cattle and sheep, and those herders were the foundation of the Pembroke Welsh Corgi.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The Pembroke is the younger of the two corgi breeds and is a separate breed from the Cardigan.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 10-12 inches and weight as up to 30 pounds (male), up to 28 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that herding work can look like a dog who wants to move people along and keep the household grouped.

Bloodhound

The job

Bloodhounds were developed to follow human scent and find people who are lost or hiding.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Bloodhounds as we know them were perfected in Western Europe about a thousand years ago.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Police departments have relied on them to follow human scent.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. In his Historia Animalium, the third-century scholar Aelian mentions a hound of unrivaled scenting powers.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The first specimens were brought from Constantinople in two strains, black and white.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 25-27 inches (male), 23-25 inches (female) and weight as 90-110 pounds (male), 80-100 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house that trailing job can look like a dog who lives through their nose and will follow a scent until they sort it out.

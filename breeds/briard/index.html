Herding Group

Briard

The job

Briards were developed in the Brie region of France to herd sheep and guard the flock.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Briards are named for the region of Brie and have worked pastures in northern France since Charlemagne's time.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. In early times Briards were used to defend their charges against wolves and poachers.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. Thomas Jefferson and the Marquis de Lafayette introduced Briards to America.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. Napoleon, who did not particularly like dogs, was a fan of the breed.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 23-27 inches (male), 22-25.5 inches (female) and weight as 55-100 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house that farm job can look like a dog who wants to herd the household and keep an eye on the yard.

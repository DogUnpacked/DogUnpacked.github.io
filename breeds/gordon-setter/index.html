Gordon Setter

The job

The Gordon Setter was developed in Scotland as a large bird dog for hard country.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Scots huntsmen bred hounds and collys into setter lines and produced a large bird dog for difficult conditions.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Alexander, Fourth Duke of Gordon, kept these dogs at Castle Gordon.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. By the early 1800s the duke's kennel was producing setters recognizable as the breed that bears his name.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The Gordon Setter's lineage is dated to 1620.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 24-27 inches (male), 23-26 inches (female) and weight as 55-80 pounds (male), 45-70 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

At home, that bird-dog work can look like a dog who wants to range through rough ground and then come back to a person. A long walk in open country gives the setter job somewhere to happen.

Flat-Coated Retriever

The job

The Flat-Coated Retriever was developed to mark fallen waterfowl and swim out to bring the bird back.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The Flat-Coat is one of the retrievers that bring back downed waterfowl.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. The dog marks where a duck falls, swims a straight line to it, and carries the bird gently.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The Flat-Coat came out of the same Labrador family as the Labrador Retriever.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. H. R. Cooke kept the breed for more than 70 years at his Riverside kennel.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 23-24.5 inches (male), 22-23.5 inches (female) and weight as 60-70 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that retrieving work can look like a dog who wants to fetch, swim, and work closely with a person. A game of fetch and time near water give the lake-hunting job a place at home.

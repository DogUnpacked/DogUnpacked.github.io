Bluetick Coonhound

The job

Blueticks were developed as scenthounds, and today's dogs specialize in trailing raccoons.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Blueticks and the other coonhound breeds are scenthounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Long ago Blueticks trailed big game, and today's dogs specialize on the raccoon.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The AKC breed standard lists height as 22-27 inches (male), 21-25 inches (female) and weight as 55-80 pounds (male), 45-65 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The American Kennel Club lists 2009 as the year this breed was recognized.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house that scent work can look like a dog who puts their nose down and follows a trail until they find what they were after.

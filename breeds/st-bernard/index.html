Saint Bernard

The job

Monks at a hospice in the Alps developed the Saint Bernard to find and rescue travelers buried in snow.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. In 1050, Bernard of Menthon founded a hospice in the Alps to help pilgrims on the way to Rome.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. The monks developed dogs able to locate and rescue travelers buried by drifts and avalanches.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. Dogs of the Great St. Bernard Pass did not carry casks of brandy around their necks.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The Saint most likely developed from heavy Asian Molosser stock brought to Switzerland by Roman armies.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 28-30 inches (males), 26-28 inches (female) and weight as 140-180 pounds (male), 120-140 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that people-finding work can look like a dog who stays near the family and wants to know where they are.

Working Group

Akita

The job

Akitas were developed in northern Japan as big-game hunters and protectors.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Akitas were developed in the Akita prefecture of northern Japan.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. They were employed as big-game hunters and protectors.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. Helen Keller was given an Akita in 1937.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. An Akita named Hachiko waited for 9 years at a Japanese train station for his master, who had died.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 26-28 inches (male), 24-26 inches (female) and weight as 100-130 pounds (male), 70-100 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house that work can look like a dog who stays close to the family and keeps a watch on the home.

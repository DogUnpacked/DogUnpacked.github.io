Working Group

Kuvasz

The job

The Kuvasz was developed to guard livestock, watching the flock and facing wolves or rustlers.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The Kuvasz came to Hungary in the Middle Ages, and the line is thought to go back to ancient Turkey and Tibet.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. As livestock guardians they watched the flock for days and were expected to face wolves or armed rustlers.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. King Matthais I is said to have trusted his Kuvs more than his palace guards.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. In the fifteenth century the Kuvasz was kept at the court of King Matthias, who claimed to trust only his Kuvasz dogs.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 28-30 inches (male), 26-28 inches (female) and weight as 100-115 pounds (male), 70-90 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that guardian work can look like a dog who keeps a long watch on the family and the yard, and who pays attention when a stranger is at the edge of the property.

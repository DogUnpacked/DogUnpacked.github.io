Chihuahua

The job

The Chihuahua was kept as a close companion.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The breed takes its name from the Mexican state of Chihuahua, where the earliest known dogs of this type were found.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Ancient paintings in Mexico show dogs that look like the modern Chihuahua.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The dogs appear in ancient stone carvings, and mummies of them have been found buried with their masters.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The breed is most likely related to the Techichis, dogs of Central American Indians, and to the Mexican hairless dog.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 5-8 inches and weight as not exceeding 6 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

At home, that companion role looks like a small dog who wants to stay near its person. A lap and a quiet place close by are the household version of a dog kept near its people.

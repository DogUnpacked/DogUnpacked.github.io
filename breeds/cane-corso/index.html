Cane Corso

The job

The Cane Corso was developed in Italy as a boar hunter, farm dog, and guardian, and the name roughly means bodyguard dog.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The mollosus dogs are named for the Molossi, an ancient Greek tribe thought to have bred big guardian dogs.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Roman legions brought mollosian dogs to Italy, bred them to native Italian dogs, and produced the Corso and the larger Neapolitan Mastiff.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The original dogs were used as boar hunters, farm dogs, and guardians.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The breed name roughly translates from Latin as bodyguard dog.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 25-27.5 inches (male), 23.5-26 inches (female) and weight as Proportionate to height.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that guardian work can look like a dog who stays near the family and notices who comes and goes. A clear place in the household, and a person to accompany, gives the old bodyguard role a daily shape.

Golden Retriever

The job

Built in the Scottish Highlands as a hunting dog for rainy weather and rugged ground.

Facts

  1. Goldens were first shown in England at the Crystal Palace show in 1908, listed as Flat Coats (Golden).

  2. Lord Tweedmouth crossed a Yellow Retriever with the Tweed Water Spaniel, a breed that is now extinct.

  3. Height 23-24 inches (male), 21.5-22.5 inches (female). Weight 65-75 pounds (male), 55-65 pounds (female).

American Temperament Test Society pass rate: 85.9% (836 dogs tested).

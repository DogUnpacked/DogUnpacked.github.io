Danish-Swedish Farmdog

The job

An all-purpose farm dog, used as a ratter, herder, hunting dog, watchdog, and companion.

Facts

  1. The Danish and Swedish kennel clubs' effort to bring the breed back produced the first breed standard in 1987.

  2. Everyday work included ratting, herding livestock, hunting, watching, and keeping the family company.

  3. Height 12.5-14.5 inches. Weight 15-20 pounds.

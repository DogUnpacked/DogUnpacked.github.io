Danish-Swedish Farmdog

The job

The Danish-Swedish Farmdog was an all-purpose farm dog, used as a ratter, herder, hunting dog, watchdog, and companion.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The dog was originally known as the Danish Pincher and was a regular part of Danish farm life.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Everyday work included ratting, herding livestock, hunting, watching, and keeping the family company.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The Danish and Swedish kennel clubs' effort to bring the breed back produced the first breed standard in 1987.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. In 1998 a dog was imported to the United States to start the first official breeding program there.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 12.5-14.5 inches and weight as 15-20 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

At home, that farm mix can look like a dog who wants to keep an eye on the household, check a small rustle, and stay in the middle of family life. A home with a bit of work to do, and people to follow, fits the old farm day.

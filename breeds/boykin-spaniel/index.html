Boykin Spaniel

The job

The Boykin Spaniel was developed in South Carolina as a small hunting spaniel.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Around 1900, Whit Boykin of Camden, South Carolina, took in a brown spaniel named Dumpy who proved to be an excellent hunter.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Boykin crossed Dumpy with various spaniel breeds and came to the gundog that bears his name.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The Boykin Spaniel is the official dog of South Carolina.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The breed is small enough that a hunter can lift both dog and duck into a boat at the same time.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 15.5-18 inches (male), 14-16.5 inches (female) and weight as 30-40 pounds (male), 25-35 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house that hunting job can look like a compact dog who wants to retrieve and stay close enough to go along in the boat.

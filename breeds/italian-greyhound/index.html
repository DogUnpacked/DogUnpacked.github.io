Italian Greyhound

The job

The Italian Greyhound was developed as a small companion.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Archaeological evidence suggests these dogs were bred as noble companions about 2,000 years ago, at the height of Roman power in the Mediterranean.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. The breed came into its own in Renaissance Italy, where miniature versions of popular breeds were a fad among the wealthy.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. It is the smallest of the gazehounds, or sighthounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. Italian Greyhounds were nearly wiped out in World War II, and American breeders far from the war kept the breed alive.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 13-15 inches and weight as 7-14 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that companion role can look like a dog who stays near the family.

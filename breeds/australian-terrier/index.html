Terrier Group

Australian Terrier

The job

Australian Terriers were bred to work on small mammals and snakes.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The breed is said to come from crosses of British terriers, including the Cairn, Dandie Dinmont, Norwich, Scottie, Skye, and Yorkie.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. They worked on small mammals and snakes.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. One of the smallest working terriers, they were bred as both helper and companion in rough times and terrain.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The Australian Terrier was the first Australian breed recognized and shown in its native land.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 10-11 inches and weight as 15-20 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house that exterminator job can look like a small dog who investigates holes and small animals, then settles in as a companion.

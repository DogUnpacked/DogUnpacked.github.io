Swedish Vallhund

The job

The Swedish Vallhund was developed as a cattle dog and all-purpose farm dog in western Sweden.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The Vallhund is thought to be a cross of Scandinavian spitz dogs and Welsh corgis.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. For centuries these dogs were cattle herders and general farmhands in western Sweden.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The breed is also called the Vastgotaspets and the Swedish Cattledog.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The AKC breed standard lists height as 12.5-13.75 inches (male), 11.5-12.75 inches (female) and weight as 20-35 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The American Kennel Club lists 2007 as the year this breed was recognized.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that cattle-dog work can look like a dog who moves with the family and tries to keep everyone going as a group.

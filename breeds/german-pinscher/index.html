German Pinscher

The job

The German Pinscher originally made its living as a ratter, seizing and nipping vermin.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The word pinscher appears to come from the French word pincer, meaning to seize or to nip.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Seizing and nipping are how the breed first earned its keep, by killing rats.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The German Pinscher is a parent of the Miniature Pinscher and the Doberman.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The German Pinscher is known for its ability to hunt vermin.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 17-20 inches and weight as 25-45 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that ratting work can look like a dog who notices small movement and wants to investigate it. A home where the dog can patrol and then settle with the family gives the old vermin job a tame shape.

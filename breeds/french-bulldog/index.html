French Bulldog

The job

The French Bulldog was bred down from the Bulldog by lacemakers in Nottingham to keep as a lapdog.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Lacemakers bred the Bulldog down to a smaller size to keep as a lapdog.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. When the Industrial Revolution took the lacemakers to France, they brought the dogs with them.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. One ancestor is the English Bulldog, most likely of the toy variety.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The breed was first called the Boule-Dog Francais.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 11-13 inches and weight as under 28 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

At home, that lapdog role looks like a dog who wants to stay close and share the room with people. A place on the couch and a person to follow from room to room is the home version of the lacemaker's companion.

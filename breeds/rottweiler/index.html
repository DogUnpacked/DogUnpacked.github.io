Working Group

Rottweiler

The job

The Rottweiler descends from Roman drover dogs used to guide cattle and guard encampments.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The Rottweiler is descended from Roman drover dogs.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. The Romans used the dogs to guide cattle, their food on the road, and to guard encampments.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. In the years 1901 to 1907, the Rottweiler again found favor as a police dog.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The AKC breed standard lists height as 24-27 inches (male), 22-25 inches (female) and weight as 95-135 pounds (male), 80-100 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The American Kennel Club lists 1931 as the year this breed was recognized.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, driving stock and guarding a camp can look like a dog who stays with the family and watches the door and the yard.

This follows from the job above.

Rottweiler

The job

The Rottweiler descends from Roman drover dogs used to guide cattle and guard encampments.

Facts

  1. In the years 1901 to 1907, the Rottweiler again found favor as a police dog.

  2. Height 24-27 inches (male), 22-25 inches (female). Weight 95-135 pounds (male), 80-100 pounds (female).

American Temperament Test Society pass rate: 85.0% (6,216 dogs tested).

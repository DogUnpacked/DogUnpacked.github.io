Facts

  1. Owners of duck clubs on both shores of the bay set the breed's basic type in the 19th century.

  2. The Chesapeake Bay holds a third of the migratory waterfowl that winter in the East.

  3. Height 23-26 inches (male), 21-24 inches (female). Weight 65-80 pounds (male), 55-70 pounds (female).

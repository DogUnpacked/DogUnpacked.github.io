Working Group

Portuguese Water Dog

The job

The Portuguese Water Dog was developed to herd fish into nets, retrieve lost tackle, and carry messages between ship and shore.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Along Portugal's coast these dogs herded fish into nets, retrieved lost tackle, and acted as messengers between ships and shore.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. They rode in trawlers from the Atlantic waters of Portugal to the cold waters off Iceland.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The old fishing job faded with new technology, but the dogs are still used for water rescue.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The Portuguese Water Dog has webbed feet.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 20-23 inches (male), 17-21 inches (female) and weight as 42-60 pounds (male), 35-50 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that water work can look like a dog who wants to swim, fetch, and stay with the family near the water.

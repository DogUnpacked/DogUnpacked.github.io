Russell Terrier

The job

The Russell Terrier was developed as a fox-working dog that could run with the hounds and go to ground to bolt prey.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The Russell and the Parson Russell share fox-working roots in the kennels of Rev. John Russell, the Sporting Parson, in the mid-1800s.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Russells were bred to run with the hounds and to be compact enough to go to ground and bolt prey.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The Russell Terrier began in England and was developed in Australia.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The AKC breed standard lists height as 10-12 inches and weight as 9-15 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The American Kennel Club lists 2012 as the year this breed was recognized.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that fox-bolting job can look like a dog who runs hard and then squeezes into tight spaces after something that has gone to ground.

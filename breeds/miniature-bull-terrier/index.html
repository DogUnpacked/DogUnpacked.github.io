Miniature Bull Terrier

The job

The Miniature Bull Terrier was developed as a smaller dog for ratting above ground.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. In the 1830s, Bulldogs were crossed with English terriers that are now extinct.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Breeders then worked on a smaller version to use as an above-ground ratter, rather than a terrier that burrows after quarry.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. A Bulldog and the extinct White English Terrier were crossed to produce the Bull and Terrier, later called the Bull Terrier.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. There were once Toy, Miniature, and Standard types, but the toy fell out of favor and only the Miniature and Standard remain.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 10-14 inches and weight as 18-28 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that ratting work can look like a dog who is curious about small movement along the floor and who likes to investigate corners.

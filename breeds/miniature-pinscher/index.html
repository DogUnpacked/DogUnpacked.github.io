Miniature Pinscher

The job

The Miniature Pinscher was developed in Germany as a small companion and watchdog.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Breed historians accept that the Min Pin is a cross of the Dachshund and the Italian Greyhound, with the German Pinscher as another possible part.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. The Miniature Pinscher is native to Germany.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. It is often mistaken for a miniature Doberman Pinscher because the names and outlines are similar.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. Despite the small size, the Miniature Pinscher is valued as a watchdog.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 10-12.5 inches and weight as 8-10 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that watchdog role can look like a small dog who notices who comes to the door and wants the household to know.

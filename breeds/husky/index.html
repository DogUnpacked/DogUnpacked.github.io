Siberian Husky

The job

The Siberian Husky was developed by the Chukchi people of northeastern Asia as an endurance sled dog and family companion.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. The Chukchi people of northeastern Asia kept these dogs as family companions and as endurance sled dogs.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. The sled dog was meant to travel long distances at a moderate speed, carrying a light load in low temperatures.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. In 1910, John "Iron Man" Johnson drove a Siberian Husky team to win the 400-mile All Alaska Sweepstakes Race.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. Leonhard Seppala both bred and raced dogs of this breed.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 21-23.5 inches (male), 20-22 inches (female) and weight as 45-60 pounds (male), 35-50 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

At home, that sled-dog work can look like a dog who wants to pull and cover ground with the family, and who lives as a companion in the house.

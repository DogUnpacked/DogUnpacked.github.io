Facts

  1. The breed descends from red foxhounds brought to America by Scottish immigrants in the late 1700s.

  2. Redbones are part of the nite hunt, a chase after the nocturnal raccoon.

  3. Height 22-27 inches (male), 21-26 inches (female). Weight 45-70 pounds.

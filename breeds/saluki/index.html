Saluki

The job

The Saluki was developed as a sighthound used to track and bring down gazelle.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Experts say the Saluki may go back as far as 7000 b.c.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Salukis were favorites of Egyptian pharaohs and of Alexander the Great.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. Arabs used the Saluki mainly to track and bring down gazelle.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The AKC breed standard lists height as 23-28 inches (male), considerably smaller (female) and weight as 40-65 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The American Kennel Club lists 1929 as the year this breed was recognized.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that coursing job can look like a sudden run on open, fenced ground and a sharp eye for movement.

Herding Group

Canaan Dog

The job

The Canaan Dog herded flocks for the ancient Israelites.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Among the ancient Israelites, shepherding was an important job.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. When the Romans drove the Israelites from the Holy Land in a.d. 70, their herding dogs took refuge in the Negev Desert.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The breed is named for the territory that is today Israel, Lebanon, and parts of the bordering countries.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. Drawings on the tombs at Beni-Hassan, from 2200 to 2000 BC, show dogs that look much like the Canaan Dog.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 20-24 inches (male), 19-23 inches (female) and weight as 45-55 pounds (male), 35-45 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

At home, that shepherding work can look like a dog who stays near its people and keeps track of the household. A family to follow, and a place to watch from, gives the old herding job a daily shape.

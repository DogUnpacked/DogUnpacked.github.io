Ibizan Hound

The job

The Ibizan Hound was used in packs to hunt rabbits on the island of Ibiza.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. About 3,000 years ago, Phoenician traders brought Egyptian hounds to the rocky island.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Islanders used packs of these hounds to hunt rabbits.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. On rugged Ibiza, food was scarce, and chasing rabbits was closer to a meal than to sport.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. Ibizan Hound history is traced to about 3400 BC.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 23.5-27.5 inches (male), 22.5-26 inches (female) and weight as 50 pounds (male), 45 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

At home, that rabbit hunting can look like a dog who wants to course and follow a moving scent. A long chance to run gives the island hunting job somewhere to go.

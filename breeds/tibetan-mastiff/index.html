Working Group

Tibetan Mastiff

The job

The Tibetan Mastiff was developed as a guardian in the Himalayas.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. For millennia these dogs were guardians of the Himalayas.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. They are thought to be the progenitor of all modern mastiffs.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. Early travelers to Tibet were sometimes given these dogs, which were then used to create mastiff breeds of the Middle East and Europe.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The AKC breed standard lists height as minimum 26 inches (male), minimum 24 inches (female) and weight as 90-150 pounds (male), 70-120 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The American Kennel Club lists 2006 as the year this breed was recognized.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that guardian role can look like watching the home and staying between the family and anything unfamiliar.

Great Dane

The job

The Great Dane was used by German nobles to hunt wild boar, and later to protect the home.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. German nobles used Danes to hunt wild boar.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Later, Danes became known as protectors of home and family.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. In Germany the breed is called the Deutsche Dog, or German dog.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The English name comes from the French grand Danois, meaning big Danish.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 30-32 inches (male), 28-30 inches (female) and weight as 140-175 pounds (male), 110-140 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

At home, that later role looks like a dog who stays with the household and notices who comes to the door. A place in the home, and a person to accompany, is the indoor version of the old protector's job.

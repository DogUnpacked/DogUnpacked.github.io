English Springer Spaniel

The job

The English Springer Spaniel was bred to find game birds, spring them from cover, and retrieve the bird.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. Springers first worked game birds alongside hunters who used nets and falcons.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. After the hunting rifle arrived in the 17th century, Springers became gundogs.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The name Springer comes from flushing, or springing, birds from their cover.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. In 1902 the Kennel Club of England recognized the English Springer as a breed separate from English Cockers.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 20 inches (male), 19 inches (female) and weight as 50 pounds (male), 40 pounds (female).

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house, that spaniel work can look like a dog who wants to quarter through grass and bring something back. A walk in rough cover, and a retrieve for a person, is the household version of the old bird job.

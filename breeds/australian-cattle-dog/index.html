Australian Cattle Dog

The job

Australian Cattle Dogs were developed to work cattle for farmers.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. In the 1800s, George Elliot bred Dingoes with Collies and sold the puppies to farmers.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. Jack and Harry Bagust later bred Dalmatians with some of those dogs.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. A sheepdog was also added to the line.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The breed was first known as the Australian Heeler and is still called the Blue or Queensland Heeler.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The AKC breed standard lists height as 18-20 inches (male), 17-19 inches (female) and weight as 35-50 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house that cattle work can look like a dog who watches movement, heels in close, and wants a job to finish.

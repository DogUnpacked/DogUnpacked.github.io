Black Russian Terrier

The job

The Black Russian Terrier was developed by the Soviet military as a multi-purpose military dog.

Source: American Kennel Club

Facts

  1. In the late 1940s the Soviet Red Star Kennel began a program to develop a multi-purpose military dog.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  2. More than 17 breeds were used, including the Giant Schnauzer, Airedale, Rottweiler, Newfoundland, and Russian Ovcharka.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  3. The first standard for the Black Terrier was published in 1958.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  4. The AKC breed standard lists height as 27-30 inches (male), 26-29 inches (female) and weight as 80-130 pounds.

    Source: American Kennel Club

  5. The American Kennel Club lists 2004 as the year this breed was recognized.

    Source: American Kennel Club

What that means at home

In a house that military-dog job can look like a large dog who wants a task, stays with their people, and watches the home.
